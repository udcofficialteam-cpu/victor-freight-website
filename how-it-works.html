Carrier stays in control

Your authority. Your decisions.

Victor Freight provides dispatch support with the carrier’s permission. No dispatch service contract or fixed-term commitment is required. The carrier remains responsible for its own operating authority, insurance, equipment, safety and compliance obligations.

The carrier decides whether to accept each load. Carriers may stop using Victor Freight at any time; they simply need to notify their assigned dispatcher so communication and any active load arrangements can be coordinated.

Common questions

Before you get started.

Do I have to sign a contract to work with Victor Freight?

No. Victor Freight does not require carriers to sign a dispatch service contract or commit to a fixed term. You are free to work with us for as long as you choose and may stop at any time. Simply inform your assigned dispatcher so they can coordinate communication and any active load arrangements.

Does Victor Freight need its own MC number to dispatch my loads?

Victor Freight provides dispatch services; it is not the motor carrier transporting the freight. The carrier provides its own operating authority details and remains the carrier responsible for the load. Victor Freight only coordinates dispatch activity with the carrier’s permission.

Who receives payment from the broker?

The carrier receives the freight payment through its own carrier or factoring payment arrangements. Victor Freight receives its dispatch fee separately from the carrier, based on the loads booked and the fee discussed for the carrier’s equipment and fleet size.

How is the dispatch fee calculated?

The fee is load-based and may vary by equipment type and fleet size. Victor Freight explains the applicable fee before a load is booked. You pay a dispatch fee only when Victor Freight books a load for you.

Do you provide 24/7 support?

Victor Freight has stated that 24/7 dispatch assistance is available, with a dedicated dispatcher assigned to each carrier. Ask the team to confirm the communication channels for your account.

Do I need to upload documents on the website?

No. The public quote form only collects initial business and equipment details. If you decide to get started, the team will explain how to securely provide carrier documents such as your MC certificate, COI, W-9 and any applicable factoring or payment documents.
