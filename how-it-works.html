Carrier stays in control

Your authority. Your decisions.

Victor Freight works as a dispatch service for authorized carriers under an agreed written relationship. The carrier remains responsible for its own operating authority, insurance, equipment, safety and compliance obligations.

Loads are discussed with the carrier, and dispatch activity must stay within the carrier's written authorization and applicable requirements.

Common questions

Before you get started.

Does Victor Freight need its own MC number to dispatch my loads?

Victor Freight is presented as a dispatch service, not as the carrier transporting the freight. The motor carrier's operating authority belongs to the carrier. The exact dispatch arrangement must be documented and comply with applicable FMCSA requirements.

Who receives payment from the broker?

Under the model described by Victor Freight, the carrier receives the load payment through its own carrier/broker or factoring arrangements. Victor Freight invoices or collects its agreed dispatch fee from the carrier according to the written agreement. Actual payment arrangements should be confirmed before service starts.

How is the dispatch fee calculated?

Fees are load-based and may vary by equipment type and fleet size. Request a quote for the terms that apply to your operation. The final fee should be stated in the dispatch agreement.

Do you provide 24/7 support?

Victor Freight has stated that 24/7 dispatch assistance is available. Ask the team to explain the communication channels and coverage arrangements for your account.

Do I need to upload documents on the website?

No. The public quote form only collects initial business and equipment details. If you proceed, the team will explain how to securely provide documents such as your MC certificate, COI, W-9 and any applicable factoring or payment documents.
