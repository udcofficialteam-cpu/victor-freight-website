Dispatch services

Dispatch support for the freight you move.

From load search and rate negotiation to broker communication and paperwork coordination, Victor Freight helps carriers manage the dispatch process.

Core services

Practical support at every stage of the load.

We work with carriers to understand the needs of their operation and coordinate dispatch services around agreed preferences.

01 / Load search

Find loads that fit your operation.

We look for available load opportunities that align with your equipment type, preferred lanes, schedule and rate-per-mile goals. Final load choices remain with the carrier.

02 / Rate negotiation

Support for better-informed load decisions.

We communicate with brokers and shippers to discuss load details and rates based on the carrier's preferences and authorization. We do not guarantee any particular rate or load volume.

03 / Paperwork coordination

Keep the admin side organized.

We assist with broker communication, carrier setup packets, rate confirmations, proof-of-delivery documents and other agreed dispatch paperwork.

04 / Dedicated support

A dispatcher who understands your preferences.

Each carrier is assigned a dispatcher who helps coordinate the agreed dispatch workflow and provides 24/7 assistance.
