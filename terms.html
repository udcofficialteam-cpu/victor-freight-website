Website information

The information on this website is provided for general informational purposes. A form submission is an inquiry only. It does not by itself authorize Victor Freight to book a load or act on a carrier’s behalf.

Dispatch services and choice

Victor Freight does not require carriers to sign a dispatch service contract or commit to a fixed term. A carrier may work with Victor Freight for as long as it chooses and may stop at any time by informing its assigned dispatcher. The carrier gives permission for dispatch activity and decides whether to accept each load. Any load already accepted and related communication should be coordinated with the assigned dispatcher.

Carrier responsibilities

The motor carrier remains responsible for its operating authority, insurance, drivers, equipment, safety obligations, regulatory compliance and final decisions about accepting loads. Dispatch services do not replace the carrier's responsibilities.

No guarantee of loads or rates

Load availability, lane options, broker terms and market rates can change. Victor Freight does not guarantee a specific number of loads, rate per mile, revenue, profit or uninterrupted freight availability.

Fees

Dispatch fees are load-based and may vary by equipment type and fleet size. Victor Freight explains the applicable fee before booking a load. The carrier pays a dispatch fee only when Victor Freight books a load for it. Carriers should contact Victor Freight if they have a question about a fee or payment.
