Privacy Policy

How information submitted through the Victor Freight website is handled.

Draft notice: This template should be reviewed and approved by Victor Freight before the website is publicly launched. It is not legal advice.

Information you submit

When you use the carrier dispatch request form, you may provide business contact details, carrier identifiers, equipment type, fleet size, dispatch preferences and information you choose to include in the message field.

How information is used

Information may be used to respond to your inquiry, discuss dispatch services, evaluate whether the requested services fit your carrier operation and communicate next steps.

Form processing

The website is designed to use Web3Forms to transmit form submissions to the dispatch email inbox. The form service may process information according to its own privacy policy and terms. Victor Freight should review the provider before enabling the form.

Do not submit sensitive documents

Do not submit Social Security numbers, bank account details, tax forms, insurance documents, voided checks or other sensitive files through the public quote form. If onboarding proceeds, ask Victor Freight for an approved secure document-sharing process.

Retention and requests

Victor Freight should establish its own retention schedule and process for requests to access, correct or delete information. Contact the company using the email address below for privacy-related questions.
