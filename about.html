About Victor Freight

Who we are

Carrier-focused support. Business-minded service.

Victor Freight is a freight dispatch service based in Overland Park, Kansas. We work with authorized motor carriers to coordinate load opportunities, communicate with brokers and shippers, and support day-to-day dispatch operations.

Our approach starts by understanding the carrier: its equipment, preferred lanes, fleet size, operating preferences and business goals.
